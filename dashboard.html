Android vs iOS Platform Architecture Notice

Android: Automated notification interception uses Android's native NotificationListenerService to detect incoming UPI alerts in real time. An APK is strictly an Android installation file.
iOS (iPhone): Apple's iOS security sandbox strictly prohibits any third-party app from reading other apps' notifications or bank SMS messages. Therefore, automated push notification interception cannot run on iOS. Merchants using iPhones can verify transactions manually via the form below or configure direct PhonePe / Paytm server webhooks under Developer API.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about direct UPI processing and checkout customization
How does 0% commission direct UPI settlement work?
SakkuPay generates dynamic NPCI-compliant UPI payment intent links and scannable QR codes connected directly to your linked VPA. When a customer pays, funds settle directly from customer bank to your merchant bank in real time via IMPS/UPI with zero intermediary holding.
How does the Android Companion App verify payments automatically?
The SakkuPay Companion App runs in the background on your Android device with Notification Access permission. When your business UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) receives a credit notification, the companion extracts the 12-digit bank UTR and amount, and sends an encrypted verification event to your SakkuPay server to settle the transaction in under 1 second.
Can I customize my payment link checkout design?
Yes! When creating or editing any payment link, you can choose from 5 professionally designed checkout templates: SakkuPay Classic (dark indigo), Minimal Clean (monochrome light), Premium Dark (onyx & emerald), Modern Gradient (cyberpunk glassmorphism), and Compact UPI (mobile-first 1-tap).
What happens if a customer submits an unverified reference number (UTR)?
When a customer submits a receipt reference, SakkuPay flags the transaction as unverified_evidence. The transaction remains strictly pending until either: 1) your Android Companion App detects the matching credit notification, or 2) you verify the funds in your banking app and approve the transaction.
Can I run SakkuPay on iOS / iPhone?
The SakkuPay Merchant Dashboard is a responsive progressive web app that runs smoothly on iOS Safari, macOS, and Windows. However, automated background notification interception is an Android-only feature because iOS does not permit third-party apps to read push notifications. iOS merchants can verify payments manually via the dashboard or using PhonePe/Paytm business webhooks.

HELP & SUPPORT

Help Center

Everything you need to know about using SakkuPay to collect UPI payments.

How do SakkuPay payment links work?

When you create a link, SakkuPay provides a shareable URL (sakkupay.in/pay/your-slug). When customers open it, they can pay using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or by scanning the dynamically generated QR code directly to your bank account.

How does the Android Companion App verify payments?

The companion app listens for standard incoming notification alerts from PhonePe Business, Google Pay for Business, Paytm Business, and bank SMS, and automatically posts the UTR and amount to your webhook endpoint to reconcile pending transactions instantly.

Why is a 10-digit mobile number required?

Your registered mobile number binds your UPI VPA to your merchant account for transparency, helps prevent impersonation, and enables our team to assist you promptly.
