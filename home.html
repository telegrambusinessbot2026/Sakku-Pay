Privacy Policy & Security Commitment

We believe in complete transparency. SakkuPay is designed from the ground up to never touch customer funds, sensitive bank passwords, or debit/credit card credentials.

🔒 1. Zero Sensitive Financial Data Storage

SakkuPay does NOT collect, process, or store credit cards, debit cards, CVV numbers, Net Banking passwords, or UPI MPINs. All payments are completed securely inside the customer's own official UPI application on their smartphone.

  • Customer money moves directly from their bank to your bank via NPCI UPI protocol.
  • No funds are ever held in escrow or intermediary accounts.

🛡 2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

We collect only the bare essential information needed to operate your merchant account:

  • Merchant Identity: Email and name provided securely via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Receiving UPI Handles: The public UPI IDs you choose to receive payments to.
  • Contact Details: Optional WhatsApp contact phone number for customer receipts.
  • Transaction Metadata: Timestamps, amounts, reference IDs, and provider UTRs.

📱 3. Android Companion App Privacy Safeguards

Our lightweight Android Companion app strictly abides by mobile privacy standards:

  • All notification parsing occurs locally on your device via regular expressions.
  • Non-payment SMS, personal chats, and contact lists are strictly ignored and never transmitted.
  • Only parsed transaction amounts, UTR reference numbers, and timestamps are forwarded over encrypted HTTPS.

🔐 4. AES-256-GCM Credential Vaulting

When linking PhonePe Business or Paytm Business accounts:

  • API secrets and salt keys are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before persistence.
  • Plaintext credentials are never exposed in browser inspect elements or client APIs.
  • You can edit or permanently delete your linked credentials with 1-click anytime.
Questions regarding our data handling practices? Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@sakkupay.in.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a registered business or GST to use SakkuPay?

No! SakkuPay works with both individual personal UPI accounts and registered merchant business accounts. You can start collecting immediately with just a Google account and a valid UPI ID.

How does SakkuPay charge 0% fees?

Traditional gateways act as aggregators who pool money into escrow and charge 2% fees for settlement. SakkuPay connects customers directly to your UPI handle via standard UPI intent links and dynamic QR codes, allowing 100% peer-to-peer settlement without intermediary overhead.

What is the difference between Direct Linking and the Companion App?

Direct Linking connects your PhonePe Business or Paytm Business merchant credentials for server-to-server webhook verification. The Companion App runs in the background on your Android phone and automatically captures incoming bank SMS/payment notifications for any bank account. Both methods update your dashboard live!

Can I integrate SakkuPay with my Telegram bot or website?

Yes! SakkuPay provides a clean REST API. Generate an API key (sk_live_...) in your dashboard to programmatically create payment links, set custom amounts, and receive webhook notifications.
