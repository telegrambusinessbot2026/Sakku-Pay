HELP & SUPPORT

Help Center

Everything you need to know about using SakkuPay to collect UPI payments.

How do SakkuPay payment links work?

When you create a link, SakkuPay provides a direct URL (`sakkupay.in/your-slug`). When customers open the link on mobile or desktop, they can pay using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or by scanning the dynamically generated QR code.

How are payments verified?

Payments go directly to your linked UPI bank account. Customers can also input their 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) on the checkout page. In the Transactions tab, you can inspect incoming payments and click "Mark Success" to reconcile.

Why is a 10-digit phone number required for UPI?

Providing the phone number registered with your UPI account ensures transparency, prevents impersonation, and allows buyers to verify payment destinations accurately.

How do I integrate SakkuPay with my website or bot?

Visit the Developers section to generate an API key. You can then make standard POST requests to `/api/integration/payment-links` to create links programmatically from Telegram bots or e-commerce stores.
